# Information Technology Institute (ITI) — Overview & Training Programs

## 1. About the Information Technology Institute (ITI)
The Information Technology Institute (ITI) is a leading national institute in Egypt operating under the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT). Established in 1993, ITI's mission is to empower Egyptian youth with cutting-edge technological skill sets, bridging the gap between academic education and the rapidly evolving global ICT market.

## 2. Flagship Training Programs

### 2.1 The 9-Month Professional Training Program (9M)
- **Target Audience**: Fresh university graduates (bachelor degree holders from any recognized university within the last 5 graduation years).
- **Format**: Intensive full-time training spanning 9 months (3 terms).
- **Curriculum**: Includes core technical training, soft skills, English language proficiency, management fundamentals, and a final real-world Graduation Project mentored by industry experts.
- **Grant Benefits**: Fully funded by MCIT (100% free tuition) with monthly stipend grants awarded to accepted students.

### 2.2 Intensive Code Camp (ICC — 3 Months)
- **Target Audience**: University graduates seeking accelerated career transition into specific tech stacks.
- **Format**: Fast-track, immersive hands-on training lasting 3 to 4 months (~480 to 600 training hours).
- **Focus**: Targeted high-demand tracks such as Full Stack Web Development (MERN / .NET / Python), Mobile App Development (Flutter / React Native), Cloud Engineering, and Software Testing.

### 2.3 University Summer Training
- **Target Audience**: Undergraduate university students in computer science, engineering, and science faculties.
- **Format**: 1-month summer workshops conducted during academic vacations across all ITI branches to introduce students to emerging technologies.

### 2.4 Mahara-Tech E-Learning Platform
- **Overview**: ITI's official online learning platform (`https://mahara-tech.gov.eg`) offering self-paced, free Arabic and English video courses covering AI, Web Development, Cybersecurity, Embedded Systems, and Freelancing skills.

## 3. Specialized Training Tracks
ITI offers specialized tracks tailored to industry market needs:
- **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science**: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, Big Data, and MLOps.
- **Software Engineering & Cloud**: Microservices, DevOps, AWS/Azure Architecture, Docker, Kubernetes, Java Enterprise, and .NET Core.
- **Cybersecurity & Infrastructure**: Network Security, Penetration Testing, SOC Operations, Ethical Hacking, and Cloud Security.
- **Embedded Systems & IoT**: Automotive Software (AUTOSAR), ARM Microcontrollers, RTOS, C++, and Internet of Things.
- **Digital Media & Design**: Game Development (Unity/Unreal), UI/UX Design, 3D Animation, and Motion Graphics.
