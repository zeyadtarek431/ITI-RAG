# ITI Admission Requirements, Selection Process & Campus Branches

## 1. Admission Eligibility Criteria
To apply for ITI training grants (9-Month Program or Intensive Code Camp), applicants must fulfill the following criteria:
- **Nationality**: Egyptian national.
- **Education**: Bachelor's degree from a recognized university or higher institute.
- **Graduation Year**: Graduated within the last 5 years (for 9-Month Program) or open/recent years (for ICC).
- **Military Status**: Completed, exempted, or postponed military service for male candidates.
- **Commitment**: Full-time dedication during the entire training duration without concurrent employment or academic registration.

## 2. Selection and Examination Stages

### Stage 1: Online Application Submission
Applicants submit their credentials, transcripts, and preferred track/branch choices via the official portal (`https://iti.gov.eg`).

### Stage 2: Computerized Entrance Examinations
- **English Language Assessment**: Evaluates reading comprehension, grammar, and vocabulary.
- **IQ & Analytical Reasoning Test**: Assesses logical reasoning, pattern recognition, and quantitative problem-solving.

### Stage 3: Technical Assessment & Personal Interview
- **Technical Exam**: Evaluates baseline knowledge in programming logic, algorithms, databases, or track-specific fundamentals.
- **Personal Interview**: A panel interview assessing communication skills, motivation, problem-solving mindset, teamwork, and commitment.

## 3. ITI Branches & Creativa Innovation Hubs
ITI operates nationwide across numerous governorates through main campuses and Digital Egypt Innovation Hubs (Creativa):

- **Smart Village HQ (Giza)**: Main headquarters housing advanced labs, AI hubs, and specialized technology centers.
- **Cairo University Branch (Giza)**: Located inside Cairo University campus.
- **Creativa Hubs in Governorates**:
  - **Alexandria**: Alexandria University branch.
  - **Mansoura**: Mansoura University Creativa Hub.
  - **Assiut**: Assiut University campus.
  - **Ismailia**: Suez Canal University campus.
  - **Menofia**: Shebin El-Kom Creativa Hub.
  - **Minya, Sohag, Aswan, Tanta, Banha, Zagazig**: Regional Creativa hubs offering full access to ITI training programs locally.
